# Potential Energy Propulsion Racer

## Project Overview
This project involved the concept generation, geometric layout, and physical prototyping of an autonomous, spring-driven vehicle engineered to traverse targeted tracking courses. The architecture leverages high-efficiency energy storage-to-kinetic transmission gear trains built entirely within predefined sizing boxes.

## Engineering Execution
* **Kinematic Modeling:** Utilized SolidWorks to design custom pitch gear profiles and lightweight wheel hubs, minimizing rotational inertia while maximizing torsional energy release from the spring pack.
* **Prototyping & Print Calibration:** Fabricated functional structural components using PLA rapid prototyping, tuning infill densities and wall thicknesses to withstand peak torque output.
* **Empirical Optimization:** Set up tracking test runs to analyze energy loss along the chassis frame, refining gear alignments with precision alignment tools to eliminate mechanical binding.
