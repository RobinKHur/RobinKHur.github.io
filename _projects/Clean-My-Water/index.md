# Xylem Biomimetic Water Filtration

## Project Overview
This initiative developed a low-cost, organic fluid cartridge system tailored for outdoor or remote field support. By routing fluids directly through the porous transport membranes found within xylem structures, the capsule filters micro-scale contaminants out without relying on commercial polymers.

## Engineering Execution
* **Structural FEA Verification:** Conducted a static stress simulation inside a unified body configuration. Applied a **250 N structural force** across the primary cylinder, securing a rigid fixture boundary constraint at the base to guarantee a structural factor of safety above 1.0.
* **Fluidic Isolation:** Calculated tight press-fit interfaces within SolidWorks to eliminate secondary bypass leaks around the wood inserts under pressurized flow conditions.
* **Experimental Benchtop Validation:** Conducted controlled laboratory testing using microfilters, lactose agar plates, and incubation cycles to track and record bacterial removal efficiency.
