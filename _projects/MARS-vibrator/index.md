title: Effect of Vibration Frequency and Tilt Angle on Flow Behavior of Granular Material
category: Modeling and Design
description: As a member of the UVA Mechatronics and Robotics Society mechanical subteam, I contributed to the design of a NASA Lunabotics competition robot focused on regolith excavation and material handling. This supporting investigation explored vibration-assisted granular flow to optimize excavation efficiency: a vibration-actuated, variable-angle inclined platform was designed and experimentally characterized to quantify how excitation frequency and tilt angle affect granular mass flow behavior using sand as a terrestrial lunar regolith analog. Steady-state conditions were analyzed through measurements of layer height, flow duration, and deposited mass across a matrix of frequency–angle inputs, providing insights for optimizing vibration-assisted excavation strategies for extraterrestrial construction.
