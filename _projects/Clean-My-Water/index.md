# Xylem Filtration System

## Executive Summary
This project highlights the engineering development of an alternative water filtration setup optimized for emergency and outdoor applications. By leveraging the microscopic structural architecture of natural plant xylem tissue, the device filters out microbiological contaminants without relying on synthetic membranes.

## Core Engineering Work
* **Geometric Modeling:** Used SolidWorks to iterate on airtight cylindrical cartridge enclosures to mount and seal the organic wood inserts securely under fluid pressures.
* **Finite Element Analysis (FEA):** Subjected the housing to structural simulation workflows. Applied a **250 N static structural load** around the main cylindrical portion with a rigid base constraint to ensure structural integrity and verification.
* **Prototyping & Fabrication:** Manufactured functional parts via PLA 3D printing for physical benchtop evaluations.
* **Empirical Validation:** Managed a strict experimental testing pipeline utilizing vacuum pump mechanics, 3nm microfilters, lactose agar plate cultures, incubation schedules, and turbidity comparisons to evaluate bacterial containment capabilities.
